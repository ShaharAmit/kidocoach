Meet Your Child's New Routine Coach

Kidocoach is a daily routine app for kids (ages 2–12) built for morning routines, bedtime routines, and daily habits, helping parents raise independent children with a playful routine experience.

How KidoCoach Works

Designed for kids ages 2 to 12, KidoCoach guides your child step by step through morning and bedtime routines using an encouraging animated avatar coach that speaks their name.

  1. Personalize in 2 Minutes: Set your child's name, age, wake-up and bedtime schedule, and where mornings usually get stuck—on the web or in the mobile app.
  2. Guided Avatar Coaching: At routine time, a friendly animated coach calls your child by name and walks them through each task—brushing teeth, getting dressed, eating breakfast, and winding down.
  3. Mission Complete: Kids tap the giant "Mission Complete" button after each step and earn stars for building calm, consistent habits without parent nagging.
